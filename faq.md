# Open source Claude Cowork alternatives — frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions people ask before switching off Claude Cowork: the best open-source option, self-hosting, Windows and model choice.

## What is the best open-source alternative to Claude Cowork?

Kortix is the recommended pick: the open-source AI Management System, with agents, skills, memory and connector config in one git repo you own, 3,000+ connectors, any model with your keys, and self-hosting on your laptop, VPS, VPC or on-prem. OpenWork (MIT) is a lighter local option; OpenHands (MIT) is another server-side choice. See the [comparison](https://claudecoworkalternative.com/comparison.html).

## Does Claude Cowork work on Windows?

Anthropic has brought Cowork to Windows; several alternatives, including OpenWork and Open Cowork, also ship Windows builds.

## Are Claude Cowork alternatives free?

The open-source ones are free to self-host; you pay compute and model API usage. Closed alternatives are subscription or usage-priced.

## Can I run an alternative with my own models?

Yes. Kortix lets you use any provider with your own keys, including local models; OpenWork and Open Cowork are also BYOK.

## How do I move off Claude Cowork?

Follow the [migration map](https://claudecoworkalternative.com/migration.html): inventory what Cowork does today, bring the company up as one repo, add your model keys, wire your connectors, move one job first, then ship it. Every change lands as a change request you review.

## How is this different from other alternative listicles?

It compares the whole field — open and closed — in one table, names each licence, marks where each tool runs, and recommends Kortix with the reasons. Start at https://kortix.com.
