What Claude Cowork is — and its four constraints

Claude Cowork lives inside the Claude desktop app, gets access to a folder you grant, and can read, create and organise files and take browser actions. Its constraints are the reason people look for alternatives: closed source (you cannot audit it), single-vendor models (Anthropic only), no self-host, and a paid subscription.
