Frequently asked questions

What is the best open-source alternative to Claude Cowork?

OpenWork (MIT) is the most visible, running locally with 50+ models via your own keys. For org-scale use, Kortix/Suna (source-available) and OpenHands (MIT) are server-side. See the comparison.

Does Claude Cowork work on Windows?

Anthropic has brought Cowork to Windows; several alternatives, including OpenWork and Open Cowork, also ship Windows builds.

Are Claude Cowork alternatives free?

The open-source ones are free to self-host; you pay compute and model API usage. Closed alternatives are subscription or usage-priced.

Can I run an alternative with my own models?

Yes — BYOK alternatives such as OpenWork, Open Cowork and Kortix/Suna accept any provider, including local models.

How is this different from other alternative listicles?

It covers the whole field (open and closed) in one table, names each licence, marks where each tool runs, and separates source-available from OSI-approved open source.
